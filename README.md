## `01` / About Me

Hey there! I'm **Hassan Rashid**, a Front-End Developer who blends **design, motion, and logic** to create engaging digital experiences.

I turn Figma designs into responsive, polished interfaces, with a focus on clean layouts, thoughtful interactions, and details that make products feel intuitive.

- 🌱 Continuously strengthening JavaScript and frontend engineering fundamentals
